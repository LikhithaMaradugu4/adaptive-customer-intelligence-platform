# ShopSphere Refund Policy

## 1. Overview and Introduction
At ShopSphere, customer satisfaction is our highest priority. We understand that sometimes a product may not meet your expectations, or an error may occur during the purchasing process. This Refund Policy is designed to be transparent, fair, and easy to understand. It covers all scenarios in which a customer may be entitled to financial reimbursement for a purchase made on our platform.

## 2. Eligibility Rules
To be eligible for a refund, the following criteria must be met:
- **Timeframe:** The refund request must be initiated within 30 days of the item's delivery date.
- **Condition:** For physical returns leading to a refund, the item must be in its original condition, unworn/unused, with all tags, accessories, and original packaging intact.
- **Original Payment Method:** Refunds can only be issued to the original payment method used for the transaction. If the original method is no longer available (e.g., a closed credit card account), the refund will be issued as ShopSphere Store Credit.
- **Proof of Purchase:** A valid order number or receipt must be provided.

## 3. Exceptions
The following items and scenarios are strictly exempt from our standard refund policy:
- **Final Sale Items:** Items marked as "Clearance" or "Final Sale" cannot be refunded unless they arrive damaged or defective.
- **Digital Goods and Software:** Downloadable software, digital gift cards, and activation codes are non-refundable once the code has been delivered.
- **Personalized/Customized Products:** Items that have been custom-made or personalized specifically for the customer.
- **User Damage:** Products that show signs of accidental damage, misuse, water damage (for non-waterproof items), or unauthorized modifications by the user.
- **Partial Kits:** Returning only a portion of a bundled product or kit will result in a pro-rated refund or rejection of the refund request.

## 4. Processing Timelines
We aim to process refunds as quickly as possible:
- **Inspection Period:** Once a returned item is received at our warehouse, our quality assurance team takes 1 to 2 business days to inspect the product.
- **Refund Initiation:** Upon approval of the inspection, the refund is initiated immediately by ShopSphere.
- **Bank Processing:** Depending on your financial institution, it typically takes 3 to 5 business days for the funds to reflect in your bank account or on your credit card statement.
- **Store Credit:** If a refund is requested as Store Credit, it is applied to your ShopSphere account instantly upon inspection approval.

## 5. Customer Support Instructions
To request and track a refund:
1. **Initiate Request:** Go to your ShopSphere account, select the order, and click "Request Refund."
2. **Provide Details:** Select the reason for the refund and upload any requested photos (required for damaged or defective items).
3. **Follow Return Steps:** If instructed to return the item, print the provided prepaid shipping label and drop the package at a designated carrier location.
4. **Monitor Status:** Use the "Refund Tracker" in your account dashboard to see real-time updates from "Requested" to "Inspected" to "Refund Issued."

## 6. Escalation Conditions
A refund case will be escalated to our Financial Resolutions Team if:
- **Delayed Refund:** A refund has been approved, but the customer has not received the funds after 14 business days.
- **Disputed Rejection:** A refund is rejected by the warehouse due to condition issues, but the customer disputes the assessment (e.g., claiming the item arrived in that condition).
- **System Errors:** A duplicate charge occurred due to a system glitch, requiring an immediate, manual, out-of-band refund without waiting for the standard batch processing.
- **Premium Members:** Any refund request from a ShopSphere Premium tier member that encounters a delay automatically escalates to a dedicated account manager.
