# ShopSphere Exchange Policy

## 1. Overview and Introduction
Getting exactly what you want is important. If you received a product but need a different size, color, or an identical replacement for a defective unit, our Exchange Policy provides a streamlined method to swap your item. ShopSphere aims to make exchanges faster than standard returns so you aren't left waiting.

## 2. Eligibility Rules
- **Timeframe:** Exchange requests must be initiated within 30 days of the original delivery date.
- **Condition Requirements:** Unless the item is defective, the product being exchanged must be in new, unworn, and unwashed condition with original tags attached.
- **Like-for-Like Exchanges:** Exchanges are generally permitted only for the exact same product in a different size or color. 
- **Defective Swaps:** If an item arrives broken or defective, it is eligible for an exact 1:1 replacement exchange.

## 3. Exceptions
- **Out of Stock:** If the desired size or color is out of stock, an exchange cannot be processed. The system will automatically default to a standard Return for a Refund.
- **Different Product Lines:** You cannot exchange a pair of headphones for a toaster. If you want a completely different product, you must return the original item and place a new, separate order.
- **Final Sale:** Clearance items marked "Final Sale" cannot be exchanged for a different color or size.
- **Price Differences:** If the new variant (e.g., a larger size) costs more than the original, the customer must pay the difference. If it costs less, the difference will be refunded to the original payment method.

## 4. Processing Timelines
- **Standard Exchange:** The new item will be processed and shipped within 2 business days *after* the warehouse receives and inspects the original item.
- **Advanced Exchange (Credit Card Hold):** For defective items, ShopSphere may offer an Advanced Exchange. We ship the new item instantly. A temporary hold is placed on your credit card and will only be charged if you fail to return the defective item within 14 days.
- **Transit Time:** Replacement items are shipped via Expedited Shipping (2 business days) at no extra cost.

## 5. Customer Support Instructions
To process an exchange:
1. **Access Portal:** Log into your ShopSphere account and view "My Orders."
2. **Start Exchange:** Select the item and click "Exchange Item."
3. **Select Variant:** Choose the new size, color, or select "Replace Defective Unit."
4. **Ship Original:** Print the provided prepaid return label, pack the original item safely, and drop it at the carrier.
5. **Track Replacement:** You will receive a new tracking number as soon as the replacement order is dispatched.

## 6. Escalation Conditions
Exchanges are escalated to the Customer Success Resolution Team when:
- **Lost in Transit:** The customer returned the item for an exchange, but the carrier lost it, stalling the release of the new item.
- **Urgent Replacements:** A premium customer requires an immediate, overnight exchange due to an upcoming event or critical business need.
- **Wrong Item Sent Again:** The fulfillment center mistakenly ships the wrong variant a second time during the exchange process.
- **Disputed Condition:** The warehouse rejects the original item upon receipt (claiming it was worn or damaged), halting the exchange process and requiring mediation.
