# ShopSphere Return Policy

## 1. Overview and Introduction
ShopSphere is committed to providing a frictionless shopping experience. If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, our comprehensive Return Policy ensures you have a clear, hassle-free path to send items back to us. We believe that returning a product should be just as easy as buying it. This document governs physical product returns across all categories.

## 2. Eligibility Rules
To qualify for a return, your request must adhere to the following rules:
- **30-Day Window:** Returns must be requested and the package scanned by the carrier within 30 days of the original delivery date.
- **Product Condition:** Items must be in new, resalable condition. Electronics must be factory reset with no personal data remaining.
- **Packaging:** The original manufacturer packaging, including manuals, warranty cards, and all accessories, must be included in the return box.
- **RMA Requirement:** All returns require a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number, which is automatically generated through our portal. Returns sent without an RMA will not be processed.

## 3. Exceptions
For health, safety, and licensing reasons, certain products cannot be returned:
- **Personal Care & Hygiene:** Intimate apparel, electric toothbrushes, razors, and cosmetics that have been opened or have the safety seal broken.
- **Perishable Goods:** Food items, supplements, and vitamins.
- **Heavy/Oversized Freight:** Items over 150 lbs require a specialized freight return process and are subject to a 15% restocking fee.
- **Hazardous Materials:** Flammable liquids or gases.
- **Gift Cards:** Physical and digital gift cards are final sale and cannot be returned for cash value.

## 4. Processing Timelines
- **RMA Generation:** Instantaneous upon submitting the return request via the ShopSphere portal.
- **Return Shipping:** Customers have 14 days from the date the RMA is generated to hand the package over to the courier.
- **Transit Time:** Varies by location, but typically takes 3-7 business days for the return package to reach our fulfillment center.
- **Warehouse Processing:** Returns are unpacked, inspected, and processed within 2 business days of arriving at our dock. You will receive an email confirmation once processing is complete.

## 5. Customer Support Instructions
Follow these steps to ensure a smooth return process:
1. **Log In:** Access your ShopSphere account and navigate to "My Orders."
2. **Select Item:** Choose the item(s) you wish to return and click "Start Return."
3. **Print Label:** Download and print the automatically generated, prepaid return shipping label and the RMA packing slip.
4. **Pack Safely:** Place the item in a sturdy shipping box, include the RMA slip inside, and securely tape the label to the outside.
5. **Drop Off:** Take the package to the nearest authorized carrier drop-off location. Retain your drop-off receipt.

## 6. Escalation Conditions
Return requests will be escalated to the Customer Success Management team when:
- **Lost Return Packages:** A returned package is scanned by the carrier but fails to reach our warehouse within 15 business days.
- **Disputed Condition Returns:** An item is returned but flagged by our warehouse as 'damaged by user' or 'missing components,' resulting in a rejected return.
- **Wrong Item Returned:** The customer accidentally returns an item not purchased from ShopSphere, requiring a manual process to send the incorrect item back to the customer.
- **Mass Returns:** A single customer attempts to return more than 5 distinct orders within a 30-day period, triggering an automatic review by our fraud and abuse team.
