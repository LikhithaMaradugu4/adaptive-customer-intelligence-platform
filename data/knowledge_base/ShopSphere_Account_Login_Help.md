# ShopSphere Account & Login Help Policy

## 1. Overview and Introduction
Your ShopSphere account is the gateway to tracking orders, managing payment methods, and accessing premium features. Protecting your personal data is paramount. The Account & Login Help Policy establishes the guidelines for account creation, access recovery, security protocols, and data management.

## 2. Eligibility Rules & Security Standards
- **Account Creation:** Users must be at least 18 years of age to create an independent ShopSphere account.
- **Password Complexity:** Passwords must be a minimum of 12 characters, including at least one uppercase letter, one number, and one special character.
- **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA):** 2FA is optional for standard accounts but mandatory for ShopSphere Premium members and for any account attempting a purchase over $1,000.
- **Account Limits:** Customers are limited to one active ShopSphere account per email address.

## 3. Exceptions
- **Guest Checkout:** Customers are not strictly required to create an account to make a purchase; they may use the Guest Checkout feature, though they will lose access to self-service order tracking and rapid returns.
- **Shared Households:** While sharing accounts is discouraged for security reasons, households may share an account. However, ShopSphere will only verify and assist the primary account holder listed on the profile.

## 4. Processing Timelines
- **Password Resets:** Automated password reset emails are dispatched instantly and should arrive within 2 minutes.
- **Account Unlocking:** Accounts locked due to multiple failed login attempts are automatically unlocked after 30 minutes.
- **Account Recovery/Verification:** Manual identity verification for lost 2FA devices or compromised accounts takes 24 to 48 hours to process.
- **Data Deletion:** GDPR or CCPA-compliant account deletion requests are processed within 30 days of the verified request.

## 5. Customer Support Instructions
If you are experiencing login issues:
1. **Forgot Password:** Click the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. Enter your email to receive a secure reset link.
2. **Locked Account:** If you trigger the security lockout by failing the password 5 times, wait 30 minutes before trying again, or use the "Unlock Account via SMS" feature.
3. **Lost 2FA Access:** If you lost your phone and cannot receive your 2FA code, click "Try another way" to use your backup codes.
4. **Contact Support:** If you have lost all access, contact support at security@shopsphere.com. You will be required to provide the last 4 digits of the most recent card used and your billing address to verify identity.

## 6. Escalation Conditions
Account access issues are escalated to our Cybersecurity & Privacy Team when:
- **Compromised Account:** A customer reports unauthorized orders placed on their account, or changes to their shipping address and email without their consent.
- **Data Privacy Requests:** A user submits a formal legal request for data exportation or deletion under privacy laws (GDPR, CCPA).
- **Persistent Login Failures:** A user is caught in an infinite login loop due to a backend database synchronization error.
- **Banned Accounts:** A customer appeals an account suspension that was triggered by our automated fraud-detection systems.
