# ShopSphere Warranty Policy

## 1. Overview and Introduction
ShopSphere stands behind the quality of the products we sell. While we retail many brands, we also want to ensure our customers are protected against manufacturing defects. The ShopSphere Warranty Policy outlines the coverage provided for items purchased on our platform, detailing the process for repairs, replacements, and claims.

## 2. Eligibility Rules
- **Standard Coverage:** All electronics, appliances, and mechanical goods sold directly by ShopSphere include a standard 1-Year Limited Manufacturer Warranty.
- **Extended Warranty (ShopSphere Protect):** Customers who purchase the "ShopSphere Protect" plan at checkout receive an additional 2 years of coverage (3 years total).
- **Proof of Purchase:** A valid ShopSphere order number or receipt is strictly required to process any warranty claim.
- **Covered Defects:** The warranty covers internal hardware failures, electrical defects, and manufacturing flaws that occur under normal, intended use.

## 3. Exceptions
The warranty is voided and does not apply in the following situations:
- **Accidental Damage:** Drops, spills, cracks, and physical trauma to the device.
- **Water Damage:** Liquid exposure on devices not explicitly rated as waterproof, or exposure beyond the IP rating limits.
- **Unauthorized Repair:** If the device has been opened, altered, or repaired by a third-party service not authorized by ShopSphere or the manufacturer.
- **Wear and Tear:** Normal degradation of the product over time, including cosmetic scratches, battery degradation (unless it drops below 50% capacity in the first 6 months), and fading.
- **Consumables:** Items like printer ink, vacuum filters, or replaceable brush heads are not covered.

## 4. Processing Timelines
- **Claim Review:** Warranty claims submitted via our portal are reviewed within 48 hours.
- **Shipping to Repair Center:** If approved, a prepaid shipping label is provided. Transit typically takes 3-5 days.
- **Repair/Replacement SLA:** Once received by our certified technicians, we guarantee a resolution (repair or replacement) within 14 business days.
- **Return Shipping:** Repaired or replacement units are shipped back via expedited 2-day delivery at no cost to the customer.

## 5. Customer Support Instructions
To file a warranty claim:
1. **Gather Documentation:** Have your order number and a clear description of the defect ready. Take a short video or photos of the issue if possible.
2. **Submit Claim:** Log into the ShopSphere Warranty Portal, select the item, and fill out the defect report.
3. **Troubleshooting:** You may be prompted to perform basic troubleshooting steps (e.g., factory reset) by our automated system or a support agent.
4. **Ship Item:** If the issue cannot be resolved remotely, pack the item securely using the provided label and drop it off at the carrier.

## 6. Escalation Conditions
Warranty claims are escalated to the Senior Technical Support Lead if:
- **SLA Breach:** The repair process exceeds the 14-business-day Service Level Agreement.
- **Recurring Defects (Lemon Policy):** A customer has to send the exact same product in for warranty repair three times for the same issue. The customer will immediately be offered a brand new replacement or full refund.
- **Safety Hazards:** The defect involves a safety issue, such as overheating, sparking, or battery swelling. This triggers immediate escalation to our Product Safety Compliance team.
