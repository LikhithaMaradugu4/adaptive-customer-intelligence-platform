# ShopSphere Shipping Policy

## 1. Overview and Introduction
Welcome to the official ShopSphere Shipping Policy. This document outlines our commitment to delivering your purchases accurately, safely, and in a timely manner. We partner with top-tier global logistics providers to ensure that whether you are ordering a small accessory or a large electronic device, it arrives in pristine condition. This policy applies to all orders placed through the ShopSphere website, mobile application, and authorized third-party channels. By placing an order, you agree to the terms laid out in this comprehensive shipping guide.

## 2. Eligibility Rules
All registered users and guest checkout users are eligible for our shipping services. 
- **Standard Shipping:** Available to all physical addresses and P.O. Boxes within the contiguous United States.
- **Expedited and Overnight Shipping:** Available only to physical addresses (no P.O. Boxes or APO/FPO addresses) within the contiguous United States.
- **International Shipping:** Available to select countries. International customers are responsible for ensuring that the imported goods comply with their local laws and regulations.
- **Order Minimums:** Free standard shipping is eligible for all orders with a subtotal exceeding $50.00 (after discounts and before taxes). 

## 3. Exceptions
While we strive to meet all shipping commitments, the following exceptions apply:
- **Weather and Acts of God:** ShopSphere is not liable for delivery delays caused by severe weather conditions, natural disasters, or other unforeseeable events outside of our control.
- **Oversized Items:** Certain oversized or heavy items (e.g., smart TVs, large appliances, furniture) are excluded from standard Expedited and Overnight shipping options and require specialized freight delivery.
- **Restricted Items:** Products containing lithium-ion batteries or other hazardous materials cannot be shipped via air freight and are restricted to standard ground shipping.
- **Holiday Periods:** During peak promotional periods (e.g., Black Friday, Cyber Monday, holiday season), standard shipping timelines may be extended by up to 3 business days.

## 4. Processing Timelines
We pride ourselves on rapid order fulfillment.
- **Order Cut-off Time:** Orders placed before 2:00 PM EST Monday through Friday are processed on the same day. Orders placed after 2:00 PM EST, or on weekends/holidays, will be processed the next business day.
- **Standard Shipping:** 3 to 5 business days after processing.
- **Expedited Shipping:** 2 business days after processing.
- **Overnight Shipping:** 1 business day after processing (delivery by 8:00 PM local time).
- **Freight Delivery:** 7 to 10 business days, requiring a scheduled delivery appointment.
- **Tracking Information:** Tracking numbers are generated and emailed to the customer within 12 hours of the package leaving our fulfillment center.

## 5. Customer Support Instructions
If you have questions regarding your shipment or need to make changes to your delivery address:
1. **Track Your Order:** Log in to your ShopSphere account, navigate to "Order History," and click "Track Package."
2. **Address Changes:** You may update your shipping address within 60 minutes of placing the order via the automated portal. After this window, the address cannot be changed.
3. **Report an Issue:** If your tracking shows as "Delivered" but you have not received the package, please wait 24 hours (as carriers sometimes scan prematurely) before contacting support.
4. Contact our support team via the Live Chat feature on our website or email shipping@shopsphere.com with your order number.

## 6. Escalation Conditions
To ensure high-priority issues are handled swiftly, shipping matters will be escalated to our Tier 2 Logistics Team under the following conditions:
- **Lost in Transit:** If a package tracking status has not updated for 5 consecutive business days.
- **High-Value Orders:** Any shipping discrepancy or loss involving an order valued over $500.
- **Failed Overnight Delivery:** If an Overnight Shipping commitment is missed, the customer is entitled to an immediate refund of the shipping cost and priority escalation to locate the package.
- **Repeated Missed Deliveries:** If a carrier fails to deliver an item after three documented attempts due to routing errors on ShopSphere's part.
