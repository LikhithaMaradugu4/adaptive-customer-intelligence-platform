# ShopSphere Payment Troubleshooting Guide

## 1. Overview and Introduction
Experiencing payment issues at checkout can be highly frustrating. The ShopSphere Payment Troubleshooting Guide is designed to help customers and support agents quickly identify, diagnose, and resolve payment failures. Our platform employs robust encryption and fraud-prevention tools to secure your data, which can occasionally result in strict payment authorizations.

## 2. Eligibility Rules & Accepted Methods
To successfully complete a purchase, customers must follow these payment rules:
- **Accepted Payment Methods:** We accept Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and ShopSphere Gift Cards.
- **Billing Information Match:** The billing address entered at checkout must exactly match the address on file with the credit card issuer.
- **Card Requirements:** The credit or debit card must be active, have sufficient funds or available credit, and support e-commerce/online transactions.

## 3. Exceptions
The following payment types and scenarios are not supported:
- **Cryptocurrency:** We currently do not accept Bitcoin or any other cryptocurrencies.
- **Wire Transfers & Checks:** Direct bank transfers, money orders, and physical checks are not accepted for retail consumer purchases.
- **Prepaid Cards:** Some prepaid debit cards or international gift cards may be rejected by our payment gateway due to an inability to perform Address Verification System (AVS) checks.

## 4. Processing Timelines & Authorization Holds
Understanding how money moves can resolve many queries:
- **Authorization Holds:** When you click "Place Order," your bank immediately places a temporary hold on the funds. This is NOT a charge.
- **Actual Charge:** The actual charge only occurs when your order is physically shipped from our warehouse.
- **Failed Transactions:** If an order fails but you see a charge, it is an authorization hold. ShopSphere voids these automatically, and your bank will release the funds within 3 to 5 business days.
- **Refund Processing:** Approved refunds take 3-5 business days to clear through payment gateways.

## 5. Customer Support Instructions (Troubleshooting Steps)
If a payment is declined, customers should follow these steps before contacting support:
1. **Check Data Entry:** Verify the card number, expiration date, and CVV code are entered correctly without spaces.
2. **Verify Billing Address:** Ensure the ZIP code matches the card's billing address perfectly.
3. **Clear Cache:** Clear your browser cache and cookies, or try an incognito window to rule out session errors.
4. **Contact the Bank:** Call the number on the back of your card to ask if they are blocking the transaction for fraud prevention (very common for high-value electronics).
5. **Contact ShopSphere:** If the bank confirms no block, contact ShopSphere Support with the specific error code displayed on the screen.

## 6. Escalation Conditions
Payment issues will be escalated to our Secure Payments Team under the following circumstances:
- **Double Charges:** A customer provides bank statements showing two distinct, settled charges for a single order.
- **Fraud Lockouts:** A customer's account is suspended due to automated anti-fraud triggers, requiring manual identity verification.
- **Gift Card Errors:** A valid ShopSphere gift card shows a zero balance despite never being used.
- **System Outages:** Multiple customers report the same payment gateway error simultaneously, indicating a site-wide infrastructure issue.
