# ShopSphere Cancellation Policy

## 1. Overview and Introduction
At ShopSphere, we process orders at lightning speed to ensure rapid delivery. Because of this efficiency, the window to cancel an order is highly restricted. The Cancellation Policy outlines exactly when and how an order can be halted before it leaves our facility, and what happens if that window is missed.

## 2. Eligibility Rules
- **The 60-Minute Window:** Customers can cancel their order without penalty or need for approval within exactly 60 minutes of placing the order.
- **Backordered Items:** If an item is on backorder and has not yet entered the fulfillment stage, it can be cancelled at any time prior to the stock arriving.
- **Pre-orders:** Pre-ordered items can be cancelled any time up to 48 hours before the official release date.
- **Self-Service:** Cancellations must ideally be performed by the customer via the automated portal to ensure the request is logged before the cutoff.

## 3. Exceptions
Orders CANNOT be cancelled under the following circumstances:
- **Shipped Orders:** Once an order status changes to "Shipped" or "Label Created," it is physically loaded onto a truck and cannot be cancelled.
- **Custom/Personalized Items:** Once production has begun on a personalized item (usually within 15 minutes of ordering), the order is locked and cannot be cancelled.
- **Digital Goods:** Gift cards and downloadable software are delivered instantly and cannot be cancelled once checkout is complete.

## 4. Processing Timelines
- **Self-Service Cancellation:** Instantaneous. The order is halted immediately.
- **Agent-Assisted Cancellation:** Takes up to 15 minutes to process if requested via live chat. (Note: If the 60-minute window closes while chatting, the agent can still force the cancellation).
- **Refund of Authorization:** When an order is successfully cancelled, the temporary authorization hold on the customer's credit card is voided instantly. It will disappear from the bank statement within 1 to 3 business days.

## 5. Customer Support Instructions
To cancel an order:
1. **Act Quickly:** Log into your ShopSphere account immediately after realizing the error.
2. **Navigate to Orders:** Go to "Order History" and locate the recent purchase.
3. **Cancel Order:** Click the "Cancel Order" button. If the button is greyed out, the cancellation window has passed.
4. **Confirm:** Select a reason for cancellation and confirm. You will receive an immediate email verifying the cancellation.
5. **Too Late?** If the order cannot be cancelled, you must wait for the item to arrive and then initiate a standard Return following our Return Policy.

## 6. Escalation Conditions
Cancellations are escalated to the Warehouse Operations Lead if:
- **System Failure:** The customer attempted to cancel within the 60-minute window, but a system glitch prevented the action, and the item shipped anyway. (The lead will intercept the package with the carrier at ShopSphere's expense).
- **Fraudulent Orders:** An order is flagged as fraud by the system after the 60-minute window, requiring a manual, forced cancellation by the security team.
- **Inventory Discrepancy:** The item was sold but is actually out of stock (ghost inventory). The system must escalate to force cancel the order and issue a deep apology and credit to the customer.
